FACTS OF THE CASE & INVESTIGATIVE SUMMARY:
Confidential field intelligence gathered by the Financial Intelligence Unit and border checkpoint intercept logs indicate that Vikram Malhotra established a multi-layered shell network under the front of Trident Holdings Ltd and Oceanic Freight Pvt Ltd.

On 2026-03-04, a suspicious international wire remittance of INR 45,00,000 was credited to Bank Account ACC-987654321 registered at Mumbai South Commercial Branch. Within 48 minutes, the capital was systematically fragmented into tranches of INR 9,50,000 and transferred to intermediary account ACC-554433221 operated by Amit Mehra.

Parallel surveillance and telecom intercept reports revealed 48 encrypted calls from burner phone +91-98201-11223 (utilized by Vikram Malhotra) to logistics phone +91-98201-22334 (assigned to Rajesh Patel) while traversing the Nhava Sheva corridor. Intercept vehicle MH-01-AB-1234 was identified transporting untagged consignment containers to Dock 4, Port of Mumbai.

Subsequent banking trace confirmed further outbound remittances totaling INR 85,00,000 sent to offshore entity Golden Horizon LLC based in Dubai.
